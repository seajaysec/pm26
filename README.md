# Power Morphicon 2026 — Consolidated Guest Roster

A single-page, consolidated view of every guest announced for **Power Morphicon 2026**
(Pasadena Convention Center, Aug 28–30, 2026), organized by the show each guest is
there to represent instead of by announcement date.

**Live site:** https://seajaysec.github.io/pm26/

## What it is

The convention publishes guests across a rolling announcement feed, a separate panel
schedule, and cumulative cancellation notices. This page merges all three into one
reference:

- Guests grouped by series — Power Rangers season by season, then Super Sentai and
  Japanese tokusatsu, other Saban shows, video games and voice acting, *Legend of the
  White Dragon*, and music.
- Cast who worked multiple seasons appear under each one, with their role for that season.
- Cancellations called out up front and struck through in place.
- Day limits (Fri only, Sat only, Sun only, Day Zero) tagged inline and collected in one list.
- A quarantine section for names still sitting in the site's navigation menu that were
  never announced for 2026.
- Promo cards for each guest, with a keyboard- and swipe-navigable lightbox.

Compiled Aug 29, 2026 from the convention's own 2026 posts, the Aug 23 panel schedule
(updated Aug 25), and the cancellation notices dated Aug 20, 24, 26 and 29. Role and
character attributions were checked against IMDb, RangerWiki and Wikipedia.

Guest lists at this event change hourly — treat this as a snapshot, not the last word.

## Structure

- `index.html` — the whole page. No build step, no dependencies; fonts come from Google
  Fonts and promo images are hotlinked from the convention's own CDN.
- `.github/workflows/pages.yml` — publishes the repo root to GitHub Pages on every push
  to `main`.

Unofficial fan-made reference. Not affiliated with Power Morphicon, Hasbro, or Toei.
